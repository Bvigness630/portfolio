A Little About Me

My Journey into Web Development

I'm a Bellevue University student working toward a career in web development. I'm building my skills through coursework and hands-on projects while learning more about creating useful, user-friendly applications.

My Story

My interest in web development began in 2022 when my dad opened his sports facility and needed a website for his business. He asked me to research how to build a website, and I decided to take on the challenge. What started as helping my dad with his business turned into something I genuinely enjoy. I liked learning how websites work and being able to create something useful that people could actually use. Since then, I've continued developing my skills through my college coursework and personal projects. I've gained experience with HTML, CSS, JavaScript, Angular, databases, and APIs. I still help maintain my dad's website today, and that experience has helped me see how the skills I'm learning can be applied to real-world needs. It's one of the reasons I'm excited to keep learning and pursue a career in web development.

I enjoy seeing a project come together, from planning how it should work to building and improving the finished product. It's fascinating watching changes to a site appear in real time as you make them. Each project gives me a chance to solve problems, learn something new, and become a better developer.

Beyond Coding

Outside of development, I enjoy spending time with family, following sports, playing video games, and hanging out with my pets. I like keeping up with my favorite teams, the Wizards and Lakers, and watching games whenever I get the chance. I also enjoy watching my brother play football and basketball, and I'm excited to watch his football career at North Dakota State. Having interests outside of school and work helps me maintain a good balance in my life. At the same time, I'm motivated to keep learning, improve my skills, and work toward my goal of building a career in web development.

Looking Ahead

I'm working toward a career where I can keep learning, contribute to real projects, and build websites and applications that are useful, accessible, and easy to use. I'm excited to keep growing my skills and gain experience in the web development field.
